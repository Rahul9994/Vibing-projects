# Holiday Gift Box
Our December best-seller: three 250 g bags, a tasting card and a brew guide, £34.

Sold 910 boxes in 2025, earning about £31,000 ([[Revenue 2025]]). 60% were bought online, 40% in the café.
Lesson learned: we under-ordered boxes and paid rush printing. For 2026 order packaging by September.
Design follows [[Brand Voice]]. 140 gift box buyers later joined the [[Subscription Program]].
