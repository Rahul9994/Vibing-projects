# Community Cupping Nights
First Thursday of every month, 7pm, free, 20 seats. Started by [[Daniel Okafor]] as Saturday tastings.

Usually full within a day. Each night we cup four coffees, often including whatever [[Priya Raman]] is testing — the [[Swiss Water Decaf Trial]] was blind-tasted here in January 2026.
About a third of attendees buy beans that night.
