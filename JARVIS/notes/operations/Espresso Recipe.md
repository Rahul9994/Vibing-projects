# Espresso Recipe
House blend "Mill Street": 60% [[Finca La Esperanza]] Colombia, 40% Brazil Cerrado natural.

Recipe: 18 g in, 38 g out, 28–30 seconds, 93°C. Grinder dialled in every morning by the opening barista.
Tasting notes: milk chocolate, hazelnut, a little red fruit. Designed by [[Priya Raman]] to work well in milk drinks, since 78% of café drinks contain milk.
Taught in the [[Barista Training Plan]].
