# Roaster Maintenance
Our roaster is a 5 kg Diedrich IR-5, bought used in 2021.

Schedule: chaff collector emptied daily, exhaust duct cleaned monthly, full service every 6 months by the engineer (£650).
It's running close to its limit at about 310 kg per week. A failed burner in October 2025 cost us four roasting days and £1,200 in repairs — a big reason for the second roaster in [[Goals 2026]].
