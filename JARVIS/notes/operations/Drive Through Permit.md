# Drive Through Permit
Plan: a pickup window on the Mill Street side for pre-ordered and quick drinks. Estimated build cost £16,000 ([[Cash Flow Forecast]]).

Status (January 2026): planning application submitted 12 December. The council has asked for a traffic survey, which costs £900. Decision expected in March.
Neighbour concern: the bakery next door worries about queuing cars. [[Daniel Okafor]] offered to share pickup space with them.
Target open date: June 2026 ([[Goals 2026]]).
