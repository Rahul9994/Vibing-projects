# Oatly Supply Issue
In November 2025 our distributor missed three oat milk deliveries in a row. We ran out twice on Saturdays.

Fix agreed by [[Tom Becker]]: keep a two-week buffer in the back store and add a second supplier (Minor Figures) as backup.
Oat milk is now 41% of milk drinks, so stock-outs hurt — complaints spiked in the [[Customer Feedback Log]].
