# Finca La Esperanza
Our main Colombian farm partner in Huila, run by the Muñoz family. Washed Caturra and Castillo, notes of red apple, panela and cocoa.

We buy about 4,200 kg per year direct, paying $5.10/lb FOB in 2025 — well above commodity price (see [[Green Coffee Prices]]).
It is 60% of the house blend in the [[Espresso Recipe]].
[[Maya Chen]] visited in February 2025; next visit is planned for harvest 2026.
