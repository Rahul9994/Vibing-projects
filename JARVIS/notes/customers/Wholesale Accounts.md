# Wholesale Accounts
14 wholesale customers buying about 120 kg per week combined.

Biggest three: The Copper Pan restaurant (22 kg/week), Brightwork co-working space (18 kg/week) and St Anne's hospital café (15 kg/week).
Wholesale price averages £19/kg. Every account gets free barista training ([[Barista Training Plan]]) and grinder servicing.
Growth is capped by roasting capacity — [[Priya Raman]] can't take more volume until the second roaster ([[Equipment Loan]]).
