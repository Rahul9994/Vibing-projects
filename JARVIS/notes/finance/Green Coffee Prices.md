# Green Coffee Prices
Green coffee is our biggest variable cost. Average landed cost went from £7.40/kg in 2024 to £9.10/kg in 2025 (+23%).

Drivers: Ethiopian prices ([[Kochere Washing Station]] up 18%), shipping, and a weak pound.
We raised café prices by 20p in September 2025 — flat white now £3.60 — which held café margin at 69%.
Direct trade with [[Finca La Esperanza]] protects us somewhat because we fix prices a year ahead.
