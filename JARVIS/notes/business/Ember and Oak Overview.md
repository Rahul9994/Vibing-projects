# Ember and Oak Overview
Ember & Oak Coffee Roasters is a small-batch roastery and café on Mill Street, founded in March 2021 by [[Maya Chen]] and [[Daniel Okafor]].
We run three revenue lines: the café (about 55% of revenue), wholesale to local restaurants and offices (30%), and online subscriptions (15%). See [[Revenue 2025]] for the numbers.

Mission: "Honest coffee, roasted this week." Every bag carries its roast date, and nothing older than 21 days is sold.

Team of nine: two founders, head roaster [[Priya Raman]], café lead [[Tom Becker]], and five part-time baristas.
Key bets for 2026 are in [[Goals 2026]]: a second roaster, a drive-through window, and doubling [[Subscription Program]] members.
