# Brand Voice
How Ember & Oak talks: warm, plain-spoken, a little nerdy about coffee, never snobby.

Do: name the farm and the farmer, give brew tips, admit when a batch was not our best.
Don't: use words like "artisanal" or "curated", or shame anyone for adding milk and sugar.

The logo is an oak leaf with a single ember. Colours are charcoal, cream and a burnt orange (#C4622D).
